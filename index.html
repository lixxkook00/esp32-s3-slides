Part 1 · The setup · finding my Mac

Calling for the ball

Before: a hardcoded IP that broke every time I moved desks. mDNS got benched.

Now: shout, listen, connect. Nothing to update when the IP changes.

Click by click. 1) GOAT Desk shouts PERCH_DISCOVER to the whole network: UDP broadcast on port 41234. Every device hears it; the phone, the TV and the printer just ignore it. "Like Ronaldo shouting for the ball across the whole pitch." 2) Only my Mac answers, PERCH_HERE with its port. The reply's sender address is the Mac's real IP, so there's nothing to look up. 3) The board opens a WebSocket to that address: cards flow in, taps flow back. If nobody answers within 3 seconds, it falls back to the saved IP. 4) Before vs now: the hardcoded IP went stale every time I moved desks, and mDNS from the ESP32 to macOS never answered reliably.

Check fast when something is happening, slowly when nothing is.

Trust nothing from the pitch

GOAT Desk only sends IDs. The Mac allowlists every URL and app.

Train every day. Poll adaptively.

The coach's whiteboard.
